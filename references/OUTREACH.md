# Outreach drafting

Use for requested B2B first-touch or follow-up drafts, not message execution. Reuse the product brief, prospect evidence, and relevant prior messages. Do not require a new campaign for a single draft.

## Message construction

Use an honest subject, a concrete and supported reason for contacting this business, a bounded offer, and one proportionate next step. Prefer a short usable message over ornamental sales language. Match the requested language and sender's real voice; preserve accurate names and technical terms.

Never invent a prior conversation, referral, customer logo, savings percentage, deadline, relationship, pain point, or proven product feature. Do not turn a prospect hypothesis into an assertion about their operations. Personalize from relevant business evidence, not intimate or sensitive personal traits.

A missing sender name, unconfirmed feature, unsupported claim, or unknown booking URL must not be silently filled. Use a clearly marked placeholder where necessary and label the draft incomplete. A draft with placeholders is not send-ready. Do not create a live link using an invented domain.

For follow-ups, inspect the existing conversation. Avoid guilt, false urgency, repeated pressure, or pretending a reply occurred. A follow-up request does not authorize a schedule, automatic resend, or a multi-touch sequence. Do not draft promotional follow-ups to someone who opted out; propose stopping instead.

## Output

Return the subject and body in a copyable block when useful. Outside the message, briefly note personalization evidence and material missing checks. Do not append an internal evidence table to the recipient-facing message unless asked.

## Fictional English example

Subject: Portable QA review notes

Hello Mina,

Your team's product brief describes weekly release reviews. Our tool exports QA checklists to Markdown, so review notes can stay with a release instead of a separate dashboard.

Would a short sample checklist be useful?

[Sender name]

Internal note: The release-review statement comes from supplied fictional material, not a live website. Sender details and contact eligibility remain unverified.
