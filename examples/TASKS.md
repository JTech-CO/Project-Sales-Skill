# Copyable tasks

Use these after loading LITE or the native skill. They are examples, not scheduled jobs.

## Product to ICP

```text
Use Project Sales to analyze the supplied product brief. Identify supported
features, two or fewer useful customer hypotheses, exclusions, and unknowns.
Do not acquire contacts or send anything.
```

## Supplied candidates to drafts

```text
Use only the attached business list and product brief. Explain fit and exclusions,
flag duplicate or unverified contacts, and draft one supported first message.
Do not infer contact permission from the CSV.
```

## Korean reply triage

```text
아래 회신과 필요한 이전 대화만 읽고 의도를 분류합니다.
실질적 관심, 예의상 회신, 부재중, 거절, 수신거부를 구분하고
근거 문장과 다음 행동을 제시합니다. 발송이나 원격 저장은 하지 않습니다.
```

## Handoff

```text
Prepare a handoff from the supplied thread. Preserve actual commitments,
unknown budget/authority, booking state, and the next owner/action.
Do not turn a proposed meeting time into a confirmed booking.
```

## Review

```text
Review the supplied campaign export with explicit cohorts, windows, and costs.
Separate replies, interest, bookings, cancellations, and held meetings.
Do not fill missing delivery data with zero or launch a new campaign.
```
